# CalAstro Support

## Quick help

**Sun and prayer times are missing.** Set a location in **Settings & methods**: search for a
city, click **Use my location**, or type coordinates.

**The times differ by a minute or two from my local timetable.** Timetables differ in their
reference point, method, rounding and safety margins. Check the method shown on the Prayer
times page (and its sources). If needed, choose your authority's method and the Asr school.

**"Up all day" / "Down all day."** At high latitudes the Sun or Moon may not rise or set on a
given day. CalAstro shows this explicitly instead of a time.

**A Solar Hijri date shows "±1".** The equinox falls so close to noon that the start of that
year cannot be decided with today's knowledge of Earth's rotation (for example 1503 SH). The
date may shift by one day.

**Predicted Islamic dates differ from my country's calendar.** Month beginnings depend on
official sighting decisions. CalAstro's predicted calendar is a calculation (Umm al-Qura rule,
or Yallop or Odeh crescent visibility), not an announcement.

**Data updates.** Turning on updates lets CalAstro download the latest Earth-rotation data
(IERS) and leap-second list. Without updates, the data packaged with the extension is used.
